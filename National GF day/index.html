Thank you for being my best friend, the best girlfriend ever, my best classmate, and just being the best person in my life.

Being my first love is something I will always be grateful for, and you will always have a special place in my heart.

I don’t really have a lot of friends, but having you made me feel like I wasn’t alone.

So once again, thank you for everything, my love.

You have made my life infinitely better, and I will always appreciate and adore you.
